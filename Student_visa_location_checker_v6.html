    General information only, not migration advice. It applies to applications made on or after 2 October 2026; applications lodged earlier and not yet decided are not affected. Confirm your situation with a registered migration agent or the Department of Home Affairs before you lodge.

    Appendices

    Legal basis: Migration Regulations 1994, Schedule 1 item 1222 and clause 500.411, as amended by the Migration Amendment (Student Visa Reform) Regulations 2026 (F2026L01347); exemption and family classes in LIN 20/158 sections 16 to 23, as amended by LIN 26/087 (F2026L01349); blocked visas in LIN 26/102 (F2026L01348). These apply to applications made on or after 2 October 2026.
